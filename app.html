Functional groups

1. Pick a bond order (always exactly one selected) and, if you're placing something, an atom/group/ring.
2. Click a blank atom to label it. To grow from an already-labeled atom, hover near it — translucent atoms appear at its open bond slots — and click one to grow there (this is also how a 4th substituent gets added).

Bonds space themselves at 120° (or 90° once an atom has 4 of them) automatically — existing bonds re-angle if needed.

Click an already-placed atom or bond to select it (Shift+click adds, double-click grabs its whole fragment, Shift+drag lassos an area — a lasso also catches any bond its box touches). Backspace deletes the selection, +/- lengthens/shortens a selected bond (a selected ring stretches all 6 sides together — click anywhere inside a ring, not just its edges, to select it), 1/2/3 sets a selected bond to Single/Double/Triple. Drag an atom or bond directly to move its whole molecule; a plain drag on empty canvas pans around instead. Cutting a bond leaves both resulting pieces exactly where they were. Undo steps back through changes.
